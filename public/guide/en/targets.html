DIETIKON · Keep your team informed

Team Targets

Monthly indicators show the whole team how it is progressing.

Service/Ticket

Enter both the team’s monthly target and its current result manually here. These values are independent of the individual podium counts. Leave unknown values blank; enter 0 only for a known zero. The TV also shows the percentage of the target achieved.

NPS and Quality

Enter current, target and previous values. NPS accepts values from −100 to 100; Quality uses percentages from 0 to 100. The trend compares current with previous. Leave an unknown value blank.

Customer waiting time

Enter time and thresholds in seconds. Green means the current value is at or below the green threshold; yellow is above green and at or below yellow; red is above yellow. The yellow threshold must not be below green.
