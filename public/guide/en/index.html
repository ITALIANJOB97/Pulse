Welcome

Pulse brings your store’s people, results and daily work to the big screen. Use the administrator area to prepare the content. The TV updates automatically; you do not need to refresh it after each save.

Start here

Sign in at http://127.0.0.1:8080/admin on the Mint PC. After an upgrade, use your existing account. The setup wizard is only for a new installation.

A simple routine

Choose the right month, week or day before editing. Enter your changes, select Save, then open the TV preview. Use the language buttons to change the administrator interface; the TV language is set in Settings.

The TV

Open TV launches the display. The pages rotate automatically. The arrow keys move between pages; the space bar pauses or resumes rotation. Close the preview to return to editing.
