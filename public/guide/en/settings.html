Settings and administrators

Control what appears on the TV and who can edit it.

Display settings

Set the store name, TV language (DE/EN/FR), duration per page and visual effects. Enable the pages you want; keep at least one enabled. Sunday can be switched on or off without deleting transfer values.

Administrators

Create named administrator accounts with a unique email and a password of at least 12 characters. An email is a login identifier; Pulse does not send email. Keep one account per person. You cannot delete your own account or the last administrator.

Help and TV access

The Help & guide link opens these pages locally, even without Internet. The guide can also be hosted separately on the blog. Open TV works directly on the Mint PC. The screen access link in Settings allows another screen on the permitted local network to view the display.

Automatic seasonal decorations

Halloween: 20 October–5 November. Christmas: 1 December–6 January. Easter: Palm Sunday–Easter Monday, calculated each year. Dates use Europe/Zurich and include both end days. Decorations switch off outside these periods, including when the TV loses its connection. Turn them off in Settings. Disable celebratory animations for static decorations; reduced-motion preferences are also respected. Historical snapshots have no seasonal decorations.

Halloween on the whole screen

Four bats fly across the entire TV on curved random routes, with a new path after every pass. Cobwebs align with the four corners of the screen and three spiders descend from above on silk threads at different times and depths. The number and size of the decorations stay limited to keep content readable. Pause with the space bar; movement also pauses when the TV tab is hidden. With celebratory animations off or reduced motion enabled, the decorations remain still.

Carnival, Christmas and Easter

Carnival adds roaming masks, side ribbons and falling confetti. Christmas has lights, snow, gifts and a flying sleigh. Easter adds bunnies, eggs, petals and roaming butterflies. Movement pauses with the space bar or a hidden TV tab.

Swiss Carnival dates vary locally. The automatic reference is Schmutziger Donnerstag through Güdisdienstag (4–9 February 2027), calculated from Easter each year. In Settings, enter start and end dates to follow the local Dietikon event. The override applies only to that year; clear both fields to restore automatic dates. Both dates must be in the same year and no more than 60 days apart. Calendar reference: Luzern Tourismus.

Temporary countdowns · GTA VI and Doomsday

In Settings → Temporary countdowns, choose each TV page independently and save. Both are enabled by default while their release dates are in the future. Changes reach an open TV during its next refresh, normally within 30 seconds.

GTA VI: 19 November 2026. Avengers: Doomsday: 16 December 2026 (Swiss cinema release). The countdown ends at 00:00 Europe/Zurich at the start of that day; this is a date countdown, not a cinema showtime.

Each page and its switch disappear automatically at expiry, including in an open Settings page and on a paused or temporarily offline TV. These events never repeat in future years and do not appear in historical snapshots. Keep at least one regular TV page enabled. No manual cleanup is needed.

23 GTA VI backgrounds and 6 Doomsday backgrounds change randomly at each visit to their page. Images are bundled locally, with dedicated pink/purple and dark green themes. They cover the screen and may be cropped to fit. Seasonal decorations are hidden on these pages. The countdown keeps running when rotation or visual effects are paused.
