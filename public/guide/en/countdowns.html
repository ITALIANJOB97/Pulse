Custom countdowns

Create TV countdowns for your team’s own events.

Create a countdown

Open Administration → Countdowns. Enter a title (up to 100 characters), an optional caption (up to 280), a future date and an optional time. Upload a background image, choose Show on TV and save. Up to 20 countdowns can be stored, including expired ones.

Title and caption appear exactly as entered in every TV language. Date formatting and countdown units follow the TV language.

Date and time

The time zone is Europe/Zurich. Without a time, the countdown ends at 00:00 at the start of the chosen day. Enter the event time if you want the page to stay visible during that day. Dates and times must be in the future, up to 2099; nonexistent dates and times skipped by the spring clock change are rejected.

Choose an image

JPEG, PNG and WebP are supported, up to 8 MB and 12 megapixels. A landscape 16:9 image works best. Images are normalized and kept locally; the background fills the TV, so edges may be cropped. On edit, leaving the image field empty keeps the current image. After an upload error, the text stays in the form; select the image again.

Edit, show, hide or delete

Each card has Edit, Show/Hide on TV and Delete actions. Visibility is also available in Settings while the countdown is active. Changes reach an open TV during its next refresh, normally within 30 seconds. A saved Settings form preserves countdowns that another administrator created after that form was opened.

Deleting removes the countdown from the list and the TV after confirmation. Previous images remain in storage and backups. Keep at least one ordinary TV page enabled.

After the deadline

The page and its visibility controls disappear automatically, even on a paused or temporarily offline TV. The record stays in Countdowns with an Expired label. To reuse it, edit it, set a new future date and choose Show on TV before saving. The creation menu always remains available.

Backups and existing countdowns

Existing backups include countdown text, dates, visibility and uploaded images. The GTA VI and Doomsday pages keep their dedicated styling and their own switches. Custom countdowns use a blue theme. Historical TV snapshots do not display countdowns. The update preserves existing accounts, team data, images and history.
