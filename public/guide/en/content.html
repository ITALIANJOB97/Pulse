DIETIKON · Keep your team informed

Daily content and images

Select the period first so your edits go into the correct month, week or day.

Monthly Roles

Choose an employee and enter their assignment in DE, EN and FR. Up to eight assignments can be saved. Unused rows can remain empty.

Transfer Tracker

Enter the completed transfers for each day and the pending total. In Settings you can hide Sunday. Its saved value is preserved, while the visible total covers Monday to Saturday.

Daily Tasks and Store Tasks

Daily Tasks provides six lines for the selected date. Store Tasks is an ongoing list: enter one task per line. Fill the languages your team uses; missing display text falls back to another available language.

Gallery

Upload a picture and enter a caption of up to 200 characters. Each image gets its own TV page. Captions wrap within the available space, and the image adapts. Delete removes an image from the current slideshow; historical snapshots keep their references.
