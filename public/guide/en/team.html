Employees and awards

Manage people once, then reuse them across the screen.

Employees

Open Employees, enter a name and role, choose a photo and save. A preview shows the whole selected photo. The supported formats are JPEG, PNG and WebP, up to 8 MB and 12 megapixels. Photos keep their proportions.

Store Team

Active employees appear together on one page, grouped as Store Manager, Deputy Store Manager, Supervisor and Store Team. The layout adapts to the number of people. For very large teams the text becomes smaller.

Employee of the month

In Content, choose the month, select an employee and add a message in the desired languages. Save and preview the award page. Effects can be disabled in Settings.

Podium

Enter each employee’s Service/Ticket count for the month. Pulse selects the top three automatically. The team target and current team result are entered separately and manually in Team Targets. Set an employee inactive to hide them from the current team; deleting is permanent.

Service/Ticket

Service Podium accepts decimal values such as 0.6, 0.8 and 1.25. Use a dot or comma as the decimal separator, without thousands separators. Values previously truncated by an older version need to be entered again.
